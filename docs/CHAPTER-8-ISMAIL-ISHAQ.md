# Chapter 8 — Ismail and Ishaq (إسماعيل وإسحاق)

The two sons of Ibrahim as prophets in their own right, after the story told in Chapter 6 (the valley,
the spring, the sacrifice and the House). Ismail grows up among the tribe by the spring, keeps his word
as a messenger, receives his father's two visits, and becomes the father of the tribes from whose line
the final messenger would come. Ishaq is born to Sarah in her old age, grows up a prophet in Canaan and
sees his son Yaqub; the chapter closes on the house of Ibrahim, one father and two lines.

**Content rules kept throughout.** God is never depicted. The prophets (Ibrahim, Ismail, Ishaq, Yaqub)
appear only as steady lights, far away; Sarah, Ismail's wives and the people as dim figures of light; no
faces. The final messenger is spoken of, never shown. Hadith details (the tribe of Jurhum, the two visits
and the threshold) are labelled in each scene's `art`. No music.

### 85 · Ismail the Messenger · إسماعيل النبي (`promise`)
- **Ayah:** 19:54. **Node:** ismail.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Ismail grew up among the tribe that had settled by the spring, learned their tongue, and married among them. (2) *Say:* God made him a messenger and a prophet, and he was known for keeping his word. (3) **Verse 19:54**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** desert, voices → voices, wind → wind, air. **Art:** Ismail appears only as a steady light, far away; the people of the valley as dim lights. That Ismail grew up among the tribe of Jurhum, learned their tongue and married among them is told in the hadith (al-Bukhari).
- **Image prompt:** A valley among dark mountains at dawn, goat-hair tents of a tribe around a stone-rimmed spring, the plain stone walls of the House; a steady white light walking among dim amber lights of the people; then the people gathered before the House listening to him; ochre, slate, rose; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 86 · The Threshold · عتبة الباب (`threshold`)
- **Ayah:** 19:55. **Node:** ismail.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Years later Ibrahim came to visit while Ismail was out hunting. Ismail's wife, not knowing him, complained of hunger and hardship. (2) *Say:* Tell him to change the threshold of his door, Ibrahim said as he left; and Ismail understood, and parted from her. (3) *Say:* On his next visit, Ismail's new wife thanked God for all they had and offered him meat and water. He prayed for blessing on them, and said: tell him to keep his threshold. (4) **Verse 19:55**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** desert, wind → wind, crickets → desert, voices → wind, air. **Art:** The two visits are told in the hadith (al-Bukhari): Ibrahim's word about the threshold of the door, and his blessing on the second household's meat and water. Ismail's wives appear only as dim figures of light; Ibrahim and Ismail as steady lights, far away.
- **Image prompt:** A goat-hair tent in a mountain valley at noon, a dim amber figure of light at its door and a steady white light, a traveller, standing before it; then the traveller walking away at dusk while another steady light returns from the hills; then a second household's tent with a warmer amber light offering a bowl, the traveller's arm raised in blessing; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 87 · The Line of Ismail · ذرية إسماعيل (`lineage`)
- **Ayah:** 2:129. **Node:** ismail.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Ismail's sons grew into tribes that spread across Arabia. (2) *Say:* Generation after generation, they kept the House their fathers had raised. (3) *Say:* Ages later, from Ismail's line, God raised the messenger Ibrahim had prayed for. (4) **Verse 2:129** (Ibrahim's prayer for a messenger from among them), placed sky.
- **Sound:** desert, voices → crowd, wind → wind, birds → air, birds. **Art:** Ismail and his sons appear only as lights. That the final messenger came from Ismail's line is the teaching of the Quran's prayer (2:129) and the hadith; he is not shown. The growing town and the caravans are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** The valley of the House at dusk, an old steady white light among several younger lights by the plain stone walls; then days and nights racing past as tents become houses and long caravans of small lights wind in through the mountain passes; then dawn over a quiet town around the House; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 88 · The Birth of Ishaq · مولد إسحاق (`isaac`)
- **Ayah:** 37:112. **Node:** ishaq.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* As the angels had promised, Sarah, old as she was, bore a son: Ishaq. (2) *Say:* Ibrahim thanked God, who had given him in his old age both Ismail and Ishaq. (3) **Verse 37:112**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** birds, air → birds, sheep → air, birds. **Art:** Sarah appears only as a dim figure of light; Ibrahim and the infant Ishaq as steady lights. The camp in Canaan is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A dark goat-hair tent among olive trees and green hills at dawn, a dim amber figure of light at its door holding a tiny new light wrapped in white, an old steady white light beside them with an arm raised; soft gold light, birds of light overhead; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 89 · Three Generations · الجد والحفيد (`grandson`)
- **Ayah:** 21:72. **Node:** ishaq.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Ishaq grew up a prophet in the land of Canaan, and in time had a son of his own, Yaqub. (2) *Say:* Ibrahim lived to see his grandson, as the angels had foretold: after Ishaq, Yaqub. (3) **Verse 21:72**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** sheep, wind → crickets, fire → crickets, air. **Art:** Ibrahim, Ishaq and Yaqub appear only as steady lights, far away. The hills, the flocks and the evening are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** Green hills of Canaan with grazing sheep, a steady white light walking with a small light beside it; then at evening under olive trees three steady lights together, the oldest seated, the youngest small; amber, olive, rose; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 90 · The House of Ibrahim · بيت إبراهيم (`servants`)
- **Ayah:** 38:45. **Node:** ishaq.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* From one father came two lines: Ismail's in the valley of the House, and Ishaq's in the land of Canaan. (2) *Say:* Both carried the same message: that God alone is to be worshipped. (3) *Say:* God called Ibrahim, Ishaq and Yaqub men of strength and of vision. (4) **Verse 38:45**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** crickets, air → crickets, sheep → wind, birds → wind, air. **Art:** The prophets appear only as steady lights, far away; the two lands, one under the other's stars, are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** Night over the valley of the House, its plain stone walls and the tents of a tribe under the stars; then night over the green hills of Canaan, a tent glowing among olive trees; then dawn breaking over a vast land between them; indigo, gold, olive; ink-wash and parchment texture.
