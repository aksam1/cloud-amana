# Chapter 1 — The Beginning (البداية)

From the beginning of the heavens and the earth to Adam's last days: the jinn, the angels told of a
khalifah, the creation of Adam and the names, Iblis's refusal and fall, the Garden, the whisper,
repentance, the descent, the first home, the children, the two offerings, the first murder, the crow,
the lesson for all humanity, and Shith. Scenes are in `src/story/chapters/c1/`.

**Rebuilt for the WebGL stage.** The chapter first ran on the flat 2D stage. Every scene now runs on
the WebGL stage of the later chapters, with its words, verses and timing unchanged: depth and camera
moves through layered paintings, living skies (dawns, noons, dusks and nights that run on the beat's own
clock), figures of light that breathe and walk, drifting motes, and new elements made for this chapter:
- *The beginning* (`cosmos`): a single thread of gold light in the dark, the heaven as swirling smoke,
  what was joined parting along a seam of light, and seven heavens as shells of light; tinted gold, the
  same element is the angels' court.
- *Ranks and rings of angels* (`crowd` `kind: "angels"`): columns of light standing in their thousands, or
  in rings round Adam, that bow in a wave and stay bowed.
- *Iblis* (`light` `kind: "ember"`): a figure of shadow in a smouldering glow, never a face.
- *The globe of lives* (`globe`): the earth's curve with every human life a light on the land, coming on in
  waves, one going out under a passing shadow, one flaring gold.

**Content rules, made stricter in the rebuild.** No light stands for God: the old scenes' central
"radiance" of the court and the beam of forgiveness are gone; His words appear only as the verse and as
words written in the air, and forgiveness is shown by the dawn and the couple's own returning glow.
Narration says God, not Allah. Iblis has no eyes or face. Habil's death is never shown: a drop of ink,
and his light is gone.

## Scenes

### 1 · The Heavens and the Earth Are Created · خلق السماوات والأرض (`heavens`)
- **Beats.** (2) *Say:* In the beginning there was no sky and no earth: only darkness, and then light. **Verse 41:11** (3) *Say:* What was joined was split apart: seven heavens above, and below them a young earth, still molten. (4) *Say:* Rows of angels, made of light, stood in silent ranks and watched. (5) *Say:* Mountains rose like pegs driven into the ground, seas filled the valleys, and clouds swept in. **Verse 78:7** (6) **Verse 21:30**
- **Made of:** clouds, cosmos, crowd, dust, earth, heavens, light, ridge, water.
- **Art:** The angels are shown as witnesses for the sake of the picture; the Quran does not describe them watching this moment. The thread of light, the smoke and the seven heavens are drawn as light and colour only; nothing in them stands for God.
- **Image prompt:** Total darkness split by a single vertical thread of golden light; smoke and matter in deep lapis blue and bronze spiralling apart into seven translucent layered heavens above and a raw molten earth below; tall columns of white-gold light standing in silent ranks along both edges of the frame; mountains rising like pegs, oceans flooding the valleys, ink-wash clouds sweeping across; the scene settling on a vast quiet world at dawn.

### 2 · The Jinn Are Created · خلق الجن (`jinn`)
- **Beats.** (2) *Say:* Before humankind, God created the jinn from a smokeless flame of fire. **Verse 55:15** (3) *Say:* They spread over mountains and deserts like fire carried on the wind, building and roaming. (4) *Say:* Their cities rose and crumbled; early commentators say they filled the earth with strife. (5) **Verse 15:27**
- **Made of:** dunes, dust, heavens, light, ridge, town.
- **Art:** The jinn are shown only as flames, never as figures. Their cities, and the strife the early commentators describe, are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** The young earth at dusk under a smouldering red sky; from the horizon a smokeless flame, blue at its core and orange at its edges, twists upward and splits into countless small flickering fire-forms spreading over mountains and desert like fire carried on wind; ghostly silhouettes of towers rising and crumbling in time-lapse; drifting embers; warm reds against dark parchment; restless, powerful, wild.

### 7 · Adam and Hawwa in the Garden · في الجنة (`garden`)

- **Art:** Adam and Hawwa appear only as two distant figures of light seen from behind. The Quran does not name the tree's fruit, so it is drawn as no particular kind.

### 11 · The Covenant · العهد الأول (`covenant`)
- **Ayah:** 7:172. **Node:** humanity.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Before any of his children were born, God brought out from Adam all the souls that would ever descend from him. (2) *Say:* He asked them: am I not your Lord? - **Sound:** air, wind → air → air. **Art:** God is not depicted in any form or light. - **Image prompt:** A wide plain under an immense night sky, a steady light kneeling alone; from him a stream of countless tiny lights rises and spreads across the heavens like a new field of stars, every one brightening together at once; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 12 · The First Home · البيت الأول (`first-home`)
