# Chapter 6 — Prophet Ibrahim (إبراهيم)

The chapter after Salih: Ibrahim among the idols of his people, his search through the star, the moon
and the sun, the broken idols, the fire made cool, the king who argued, the journey west, and the
honoured guests who go on toward the cities of Lut (Chapter 7). The valley of Makkah, the sacrifice and
the House belong to Ismail's chapter (Chapter 8). Scenes are in `src/story/chapters/c6/`; image prompts
follow the shared Troy + Three Kingdoms style (`npm run prompts` adds the style and the content rules).

**Content rules kept throughout.** God is never depicted, in any form or light: the fire turns cool on
screen, and His words appear only as the verse. Ibrahim, Lut and the angels appear only as light, far away
or from behind; Sarah, Azar, the king and the people as dim figures of light; no faces. Idols are carved
stone and wood: they may be shown, and broken, but never honoured by the camera. Nothing violent is shown:
Ibrahim goes into the fire and is not harmed. No music.

**New in the engine for this chapter.**
- *A living sky* (`heavens`): a time-lapse sky whose colours run through night, dawn, day and dusk on the
  beat's own clock, with a star, the moon and the sun rising and setting along real arcs behind the hills,
  and the whole scene's light and haze following the time of day.
- *Shattering cards* (`shatter`): a painted idol breaks into shards that tumble and fall.
- *A shader fire* (`fire`): animated ink-wash flames with dark ink at their edges, sparks and heat, which
  can turn cool (`cool`), silver-blue and calm.

## Scenes

### 59 · The City of Idols · مدينة الأصنام (`ur`)
- **Ayah:** 6:74. **Engine:** the ziggurat painter; a crowd streaming to the temple.
- **Beats.** (1) Dawn over a river city of mud brick, a stepped temple tower rising above it, crowds going
  up its ramps. *Say:* Long after Thamud, in a city between great rivers, people bowed to idols of stone
  and wood. (2) Inside a workshop: rows of carved figures, a dim figure at work. *Say:* Ibrahim's own father,
  Azar, carved them with his hands. (3) A young steady light stands among the idols, still. *Say:* But
  Ibrahim, his son, could not bow to what hands had made. (4) **Verse 6:74.**
- **Sound:** water, city → work → air. **Art:** the city and its temple tower are imagined after the
  ziggurats of Mesopotamia; the people appear as dim lights.
- **Image prompt:** Dawn over a city of sun-baked mud brick between two broad rivers, palm groves along
  the banks, a vast stepped temple tower rising from its heart with long ramps where streams of dim amber
  lights climb toward the summit; inside a workshop, rows of carved stone and wooden figures with tall
  headdresses and blank faces stand in ranks, a dim figure bent at work among them, and a single young
  steady white light standing apart; dust in slanting light; ochre, brick red, river blue and gold;
  ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 60 · The Star, the Moon and the Sun · النجم والقمر والشمس (`heavens`)
- **Ayah:** 6:79. **Engine:** the living sky, end to end.
- **Beats.** (1) Night falls over the plain; a bright star rises; Ibrahim's light on a hill, from behind.
  *Say:* When night covered him, he saw a star rise, and said: is this my lord? But it set, and he said: I
  do not love things that set. (2) The moon rises, full and bright, crosses, and sinks. *Say:* Then he saw the
  moon rising, and it too went down. (3) Dawn: the sun rises, the land floods with gold, the sun crosses
  and sets. *Say:* Then the sun, the greatest of them, and it set as well. (4) **Verse 6:79**, at dusk.
- **Sound:** crickets, air → wind → birds. **Art:** the hill and the plain are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A lone steady white light seen from behind on a rocky hill above a dark plain; the
  night sky deep indigo with countless stars, one bright star rising and setting; then a great full moon
  rising silver over the hills and sinking again; then dawn flooding the plain with gold as the sun climbs,
  crosses the sky and sets in crimson; the whole sky changing in one continuous time-lapse; indigo, silver,
  gold and crimson; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 61 · My Father · حوار مع الأب (`father`)
- **Ayah:** 19:47. **Engine:** two lights in a lamplit room; a door of daylight.
- **Beats.** (1) Lamplit workshop: Ibrahim's light before his father's dim figure. *Say:* Gently, Ibrahim
  asked his father why he worshipped what could neither hear nor see. (2) The father turns away, angry.
  *Say:* His father turned on him in anger and told him to go away. (3) Ibrahim's light walks out into the
  daylight. **Verse 19:47.**
- **Sound:** fire (small lamp), voice → air. **Art:** the scene is imagined from 19:42–47.
- **Image prompt:** A dim workshop of carved idols lit by a single oil lamp; a steady white light standing
  before a dim amber figure seated among the idols; the amber figure turning away; then the white light
  walking out through a doorway into bright daylight; warm lamp amber, deep umber shadows, a slice of white
  sun; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 62 · The Broken Idols · تحطيم الأصنام (`broken`)
- **Ayah:** 21:58. **Engine:** shattering cards.
- **Beats.** (1) The people stream out of the city to their festival. *Say:* On the day of their festival
  the people left the city, and Ibrahim stayed behind. (2) The temple hall, empty, idols in rows. *Say:* He
  went into the temple, where the idols stood in silence. (3) The idols break apart, one after another,
  all but the largest. *Say:* He broke them to pieces, all but the largest of them. (4) **Verse 21:58.**
- **Sound:** crowd, voices → echo → stone, drop → air. **Art:** the axe left on the largest idol is from
  the commentaries and is not shown.
- **Image prompt:** A vast temple hall of mud brick and cedar beams, shafts of light falling through high
  openings onto rows of carved idols with tall headdresses and blank faces; the idols cracking and breaking
  into tumbling shards of stone and painted wood, dust rising in the light beams, only the largest idol left
  standing at the end of the hall; ochre, faded lapis and gilt, stone grey; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 63 · Ask Them · المواجهة (`ask`)
- **Ayah:** 21:63. **Engine:** jeers swirling, then falling silent.
- **Beats.** (1) The crowd returns and finds the broken idols. *Say:* When they returned and saw what had
  been done, they brought Ibrahim before everyone. (2) Jagged strokes of anger swirl around his light.
  *Say:* Was it you who did this to our gods, they asked. (3) The strokes fall still; the crowd lowers.
  **Verse 21:63.**
- **Sound:** crowd, jeer → jeer → air. **Art:** the crowd's anger is jagged strokes, never words.
- **Image prompt:** The temple court crowded with figures of dim amber light around a single steady white
  light, jagged dark-red strokes of anger swirling around it; behind, the broken idols lie in shards before
  the one great idol still standing; then the strokes falling away and the crowd's lights lowering in
  silence; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 64 · The Fire · النار (`fire`)
- **Ayah:** 21:69. **Engine:** the shader fire and its cool transform.
- **Beats.** (1) An immense fire roars in a walled pit, smoke and sparks rising. *Say:* They built a fire
  so great that its heat kept them far away, and cast Ibrahim into it. (2) His light goes down into the
  flames. *Say:* But the fire did not burn him. (3) The flames turn silver and cool; light settles. **Verse
  21:69.**
- **Sound:** roar, fire → fire → air, spring. **Art:** how he was cast in is from the commentaries and is
  not shown; the fire's turning cool is drawn from the verse; nothing violent is shown.
- **Image prompt:** A colossal fire in a walled pit at night, flames like ink-wash tongues of orange and
  crimson leaping higher than the walls, sparks streaming into a black sky, a vast crowd of dim lights held
  far back by the heat; a single steady white light descending into the heart of the flames; then the fire
  turning silver and cool, its tongues slowing into calm pale light, the white light resting at its centre
  unharmed; ember red turning to silver-blue; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 65 · The King Who Argued · الملك المجادل (`king`)
- **Ayah:** 2:258 (the part with the sun). **Engine:** the living sky at dawn.
- **Beats.** (1) A throne hall of brick and lapis; a dark figure on the throne. *Say:* A king claimed that
  he, too, gave life and death. (2) Through the hall's great doorway, the sun rising in the east. *Say:*
  Ibrahim answered: my Lord brings the sun up from the east; bring it up from the west. (3) **Verse 2:258**,
  the king silent.
- **Sound:** echo, voice → air → air. **Art:** the king is traditionally named Nimrod; he appears only as a
  dark figure, and the hall is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A towering throne hall of mud brick and glazed lapis tiles, ranks of columns, a dark
  figure seated high on a stepped throne; a small steady white light standing far below before it; through
  the great doorway behind, the sun rising red-gold in the east and pouring a long beam of light across the
  floor; lapis blue, gold, deep shadow; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 66 · The Journey · الهجرة (`journey`)
- **Ayah:** 37:99. **Engine:** a long montage of lands in one flight.
- **Beats.** (1) Three lights leave the river city at dawn. *Say:* Ibrahim left his people, and with him
  went his wife Sarah and Lut, who believed in him. (2) Across steppe, river crossings and mountains.
  *Say:* They travelled far toward the west, trusting God to guide them. (3) Green hills of a new land at
  evening. **Verse 37:99.**
- **Sound:** water → wind, desert → birds. **Art:** the route is imagined; Sarah and Lut appear as lights.
- **Image prompt:** A short line of three steady lights leaving a walled river city at dawn; then crossing
  a vast grassy steppe, fording a broad river, climbing a pass between blue mountains, the lights tiny in
  immense landscapes; then green hills with olive trees under a golden evening sky; ink-wash and parchment
  texture.

### 67 · The Honoured Guests · الضيوف (`guests`)
- **Ayah:** 51:28. **Engine:** angels as radiance; lamplight and dusk.
- **Beats.** (1) Evening at Ibrahim's tent among the hills; three tall radiances approach. *Say:* One
  evening three guests came to Ibrahim's tent, and he hurried to bring them a roasted calf. (2) The food
  untouched; the tent's light trembles. *Say:* But their hands did not reach for it, and he grew afraid.
  (3) Their light opens wide and calm. **Verse 51:28.**
- **Sound:** sheep, air → fire → air. **Art:** the guests are angels and appear only as radiance; the meal
  is told, not shown.
- **Image prompt:** A dark goat-hair tent among rolling hills at dusk, a small fire before it, a steady
  white light standing at its door; three tall figures made only of white radiance approaching across the
  grass, soft light like folded wings around them; then all of them seated in a ring of calm light; dusk
  violet, lamp amber, pure white; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 68 · Toward the Cities of Lut · نحو مدن لوط (`toward`)
- **Ayah:** 51:31–32. **Engine:** radiance departing along a valley toward a dead sea.
- **Beats.** (1) Dawn: Ibrahim's light walks with the guests to a ridge. *Say:* At dawn Ibrahim walked
  with his guests and asked them where they were going. (2) From the ridge, far below, a still sea and the
  cities by it. *Say:* They were going on, they said, to the people of Lut. (3) The radiances descend
  toward the cities. **Verse 51:31–32.**
- **Sound:** wind, air → air → wind.
- **Image prompt:** A ridge at dawn above a deep valley, a steady white light standing with three tall
  radiances; far below, a still, dark, mineral sea and walled cities along its shore under a bruised sky;
  the radiances descending the valley toward the cities, the white light remaining on the ridge; ink-wash
  and parchment texture.

## What the engine shows and what it approximates

- Sky time-lapses compress hours into seconds; bodies move along simple arcs, not true orbits.
- Shards are cut from the painted idol; they tumble without colliding.
- Flames are painted noise, not a fluid simulation; the cool fire is a colour and motion change.
