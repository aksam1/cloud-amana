# Chapter 9 — Yaqub and Yusuf (يعقوب ويوسف)

The house of Yaqub in Canaan and the story the Quran calls the best of stories: Yusuf's dream of eleven
stars, the well, the caravan, the house of the Aziz, the torn shirt, the prison, the king's dream and the
storehouses, the brothers in Egypt, Yaqub's patient grief, and the dream fulfilled when the family is
reunited.

**Content rules kept throughout.** God is never depicted. The prophets (Yaqub, Yusuf) appear only as
steady lights; the brothers, the wife of the Aziz, the king and the people as dim figures of light; no
faces. The temptation is told in narration only (a run to the door); nothing of the well, the prison or
the grief is shown as suffering. The dream's stars, sun and moon only dip toward Yusuf's light. Imagined
and commentary details are labelled in each scene's `art`. No music.

**Scale.** Everything stands at true size: a person of 1.7 m is 255 × depth design units tall (a 1 m
brazier, a 1.8 m well curb, a 2 m camel's hump, a 2.6 m tent), and each scene's people and props are
brought nearer the camera together so they read clearly.

**Verses.** Every verse beat waits as `pendingVerse` (placed sky) until api.alquran.cloud can be reached;
12:18, 12:23, 12:76 and 12:100 are parts of ayat whose words are chosen by hand once fetched.

## Scenes

### 92 · The House of Yaqub · بيت يعقوب (`household`)
- **Ayah:** 2:132. **Node:** yaqub.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Ibrahim's grandson Yaqub, also called Israel, lived in the land of Canaan with his twelve sons. (2) *Say:* He loved the two youngest most, Yusuf and Binyamin, and the older brothers saw it. (3) **Verse 2:132**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** sheep, air → crickets, fire → crickets, air. **Art:** Yaqub and the young Yusuf appear only as steady lights, the brothers as dim figures of light. That Yusuf and Binyamin were the sons of Rahil is told in the commentaries.
- **Image prompt:** Goat-hair tents among olive trees and green hills of Canaan at evening, a steady white light seated before the largest tent, a small bright light and a smaller one close beside him, and ten dim amber figures of light standing a little apart; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 93 · Eleven Stars · حلم يوسف (`stars`)
- **Ayah:** 12:4. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* One night the boy Yusuf had a dream. (2) *Say:* He saw eleven stars, and the sun and the moon, (3) *Say:* and they were bowing down to him. His father told him to keep the dream from his brothers. (4) **Verse 12:4**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** crickets, air → air → air → birds, air. **Art:** Yusuf and Yaqub appear only as steady lights. The dream's stars, sun and moon are shown as lights that bow by dipping toward him, nothing more.
- **Image prompt:** A goat-hair tent under a night sky, a small light asleep beside it; then a dream sky of deep violet smoke where eleven stars, a golden sun and a silver moon hang in an arc and slowly dip toward a small steady light below; then morning, an older steady light beside the boy; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 94 · The Well · البئر (`well`)
- **Ayah:** 12:18. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Jealous, the brothers took Yusuf out with them to the pastures, and threw him into a well. (2) *Say:* Alone in the dark, he was inspired that one day he would tell them of what they had done. (3) *Say:* At nightfall they came home weeping, with his shirt, and said a wolf had eaten him. (4) **Verse 12:18**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** desert, wind → wind → crickets, voices → crickets, air. **Art:** Yusuf appears only as a steady light; his brothers as dim figures of light. Nothing is shown of how he was put into the well; the shirt is told, not shown.
- **Image prompt:** Dry hills at noon, a stone-rimmed well under a lone thorn tree, dim amber figures leading a small steady light toward it; then the dark inside of a well, a disc of sky far above and the small light kneeling below; then night at a tent, the dim figures returning to an old steady light; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 95 · The Caravan · القافلة (`caravan`)
- **Ayah:** 12:20. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* A caravan stopped at the well and sent a man to draw water. (2) *Say:* Instead of water he drew up a boy, and they hid him to sell as goods. (3) *Say:* In Egypt they sold him cheaply, for a few coins. (4) **Verse 12:20**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** desert, herd → desert, voices → wind, herd → wind. **Art:** Yusuf appears only as a steady light; the travellers as dim figures of light. The caravan and its road are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A stone-rimmed well in dry hills, a caravan of camels resting beside it, a dim figure drawing up a rope and with it a small steady light; then the caravan crossing great golden dunes toward Egypt under a high sun; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 97 · The Torn Shirt · القميص الممزق (`torn`)

- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* The wife of the Aziz desired him. She closed the doors, but he refused: I seek refuge in God. (2) *Say:* He ran for the door; she caught his shirt from behind, and it tore. (3) *Say:* Her husband stood in the doorway. A witness said: if the shirt is torn at the back, he is telling the truth. It was. (4) **Verse 12:23**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** echo, fire → echo → echo, voices → echo, air. **Art:** Yusuf appears only as a steady light; the Aziz, his wife and the witness as dim figures of light. Nothing more than the run to the door is shown; the shirt is told, not shown.
- **Image prompt:** A lamplit hall of a palace with tall columns and closed doors, a dim amber figure of light and a steady white light; the white light hurrying toward a lit doorway, the amber light behind; in the doorway a third dim figure; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 98 · The Women of the City · مجلس نساء المدينة (`banquet`)
- **Ayah:** 12:33. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Soon the women of the city were gossiping: the Aziz's wife has fallen for her young servant! So she invited them to a banquet, and gave each a knife to cut her fruit. (2) *Say:* Then she called Yusuf in. When they saw him, they were so astonished that they cut their own hands without noticing, and said: this is no mortal man, this is a noble angel! (3) *Say:* Still she threatened him with prison if he would not obey her. Yusuf prayed: my Lord, prison is dearer to me than what they call me to. (4) **Verse 12:33**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** echo, voices → echo → echo, air → echo, air. **Art:** Yusuf appears only as a steady light; the women as dim figures of light. Their cut hands are told in words, never shown. The banquet's look is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A lamplit hall in a great Egyptian house, women's dim rose-gold lights seated on cushions in a long row, a bowl of fruit and a small knife before each; jagged strokes of gossip rising; then a steady white light entering through a doorway and every seated light flaring in astonishment; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 99 · Two Companions in Prison · السجن (`prison`)
- **Ayah:** 12:39. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Though he had done no wrong, Yusuf was put in prison, and two young men were imprisoned with him. (2) *Say:* One dreamed he was pressing wine; the other, that birds ate bread from his head. Before explaining their dreams, Yusuf spoke to them of God. (3) **Verse 12:39**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** echo, drips → echo, voice → echo, air. **Art:** Yusuf appears only as a steady light; his fellow prisoners as dim figures of light. The prison is imagined; their dreams are told, not shown.
- **Image prompt:** A bare stone cell, a single shaft of daylight falling from a high window, a steady white light kneeling in it and two dim amber figures of light listening; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 100 · The King's Dream · رؤيا الملك (`cows`)
- **Ayah:** 12:43. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* The king of Egypt dreamed a dream that no one in his court could explain. (2) *Say:* Seven fat cows were eaten by seven lean ones; (3) *Say:* and there were seven green ears of grain, and seven dry. (4) **Verse 12:43**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** echo, voices → air → air, wind → air. **Art:** The king appears only as a dim figure of light. The dream's cows and ears of grain are drawn in ink as he described them.
- **Image prompt:** A lapis throne hall where a dim figure sits troubled; then a dream of violet mist where seven sleek cows are followed by seven lean ones, their ribs showing; then seven green ears of grain beside seven withered ones; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 101 · Innocence Declared · ظهور البراءة (`innocence`)
- **Ayah:** 12:51. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* The king sent for the man who had explained his dream. But Yusuf would not leave prison until his name was cleared: ask what happened with the women who cut their hands. (2) *Say:* The women said: God forbid! We know no evil of him. And the Aziz's wife confessed: now the truth is out. It was I who tried to seduce him, and he is truthful. (3) **Verse 12:51**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** echo, voices → echo → echo, air. **Art:** The king, the women and the Aziz's wife appear only as dim figures of light. Yusuf's light is far off in the prison; his answer is told.
- **Image prompt:** The king's lapis throne hall, a dim seated figure on a low dais, a row of dim rose-gold lights of women before him; one of them stepping forward alone and bowing her head as she speaks; far away through a high window, a steady light in a dark prison; ink-wash and parchment texture.

(2) *Say:* Through the good years the granaries rose and filled. (3) *Say:* Then the famine came, and people travelled from far lands to buy grain in Egypt. (4) **Verse 12:55**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, birds → work, wind → wind, crowd → wind. **Art:** Yusuf appears only as a steady light; the people as dim figures of light. The granaries and fields are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** Golden wheat fields along a broad river under a high sun, tall granaries of pale stone rising one by one, a steady white light overseeing dim workers; then the fields dry and cracked, long lines of travellers' lights coming for grain; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 105 · The Dream Fulfilled · لقاء يوسف وأبيه (`reunion`)
- **Ayah:** 12:100. **Node:** yusuf.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* At last he told them: I am Yusuf. And he forgave them: no blame on you today. (2) *Say:* He sent his shirt to his father. Laid over Yaqub's face, it gave him back his sight. (3) *Say:* The whole family came to Egypt. Yusuf seated his parents beside him, and his brothers bowed: the dream of the eleven stars, the sun and the moon had come true. My Lord, Yusuf prayed, let me die in submission to You, and join me with the righteous. (4) **Verse 12:100**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** echo, voices → birds, air → echo, voices → echo, air. **Art:** Yusuf and Yaqub appear only as steady lights; the family as dim figures of light. Their bowing was the greeting of their time, as the Quran tells it.
- **Image prompt:** An old steady light seated before a tent at dawn, a shirt of white light laid over him, his glow brightening; then a lapis palace hall, a steady white light on the dais with two lights seated beside him, eleven dim amber lights bowed low before them; the dream of the eleven stars, the sun and the moon fulfilled; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 106 · Yaqub's Last Words · وصية يعقوب (`lastwords`)
- **Ayah:** 2:133. **Node:** yaqub.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Yaqub lived out his last years in Egypt with all his family around him. When death came near, he gathered his sons and asked them: what will you worship after me? (2) *Say:* They answered: we will worship your God, the God of your fathers Ibrahim, Ismail and Ishaq: one God, and to Him we submit. (3) *Say:* So the Children of Israel came to live in Egypt. Generations later, their descendants would cry out under a Pharaoh who had forgotten Yusuf. (4) **Verse 2:133**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** air, crickets → air → air, wind → air. **Art:** Yaqub and Yusuf appear only as steady lights; his other sons as dim figures of light. That Yaqub died in Egypt is from the commentaries; his death is shown only as his light growing still and rising.
- **Image prompt:** A quiet room of an Egyptian house at dusk, an oil lamp burning; an old steady light lying on a low bed, twelve figures of light kneeling close around him, one of them steady and clear; then the old light rising gently and fading through the window into the evening sky; ink-wash and parchment texture.
