# Chapter 21 — The Day of Judgment (يوم الدين)

The Hour itself, in the pictures the Quran uses for it: the earth levelled and pounded, the mountains fluffed
like wool, the people like scattered moths, the scales weighed so that an atom's weight of good or of evil
will be seen, the keepers who record, the soul at the collar bones, the wrongdoer's wish to be sent back, and
the command that is entirely with God.

**Content rules kept throughout.** Nothing of the Day is shown. The earth opens; the mountains lie down
like wool; the people are points of light drifting like moths; a light at the collar bones dims and goes out;
the scales are never drawn. Every life on the globe is a point of light, as the Quran says of the earth (5:32),
but what each life carried is not shown, because only God knows it. There is no suffering on screen and no
violence: the dying is a light going out, and the earth being crushed is the ground cracking.

**God is never depicted**, in any form or light, and never stood for. 82:19 says the command on that Day is
entirely with God; the scene shows the closed horizon and a small warm light standing at it, and nothing
more. No person appears in these seven scenes; there is no crowd, no prophet and no antagonist to put out.

**The sky does the work.** All seven scenes run on the `heavens` layer — a living sky that carries the hour
on each beat's clock and drives the light. The judgment scenes hold deep night (`sun: null`); the one dawn
scene (`moths`) lets the sun come up low on the left; `keepers` and `soul` sit at first light and at the end
of the day. Because the sky drives the scene's light, a night sky switches the sun off with `"sun": null`
rather than dimming it.

**Verses.** 89:21-22, 101:4-5, 99:7-8, 82:10-12, 75:13-14, 75:26-28 and 82:19, all on file in
`src/data/ayahs.json` and shown in Arabic and in Sahih International's English, verbatim. Several are parts
of a longer ayah, with the word range chosen by hand.

## Scenes

### 197 · The Earth Is Leveled · دك الأرض (`earth`)
- **Ayah:** 89:21-22. **Node:** judgment.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* The Quran describes the Day of Judgment in pictures rather than in a report. First the ground: when the earth is leveled, pounded and crushed. (2) *Say:* The seams open, and out of them rise the lights of those who were given back. Nothing is said of what they carried; that is known only to God. Then the ground closes over, and the plain is left level and perfectly still. (3) **Verse 89:21-22**, placed lower.
- **Sound:** wind, air → air → air. **Art:** 89:21-22: when the earth is leveled, pounded and crushed, and your Lord has come and the angels, rank upon rank. The scene shows the earth only: the coming of the Lord and the angels is not depicted. Every life on the globe is a point of light (5:32); what each life carried is not shown, because only God knows it. The look of the cracked earth is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** The earth seen from above as it splits open in long seams, the ground giving up its dead as points of light rise out of the fissures, a vast plain left bare behind them; then the seams closing and the plain left perfectly level and still; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 198 · Like Moths · الفراشات (`moths`)
- **Ayah:** 101:4-5. **Node:** judgment.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* On the Day of the Striking Calamity, the Quran says, people will be like moths, dispersed. And the mountains will be like wool, fluffed up. (2) *Say:* The mountains stand pale and soft, and the lights of people drift and scatter through the air like moths. Then the scales are weighed. The Quran says no more about the weighing than that it happens. (3) **Verse 101:4-5**, placed lower.
- **Sound:** wind, air → air, wind → air. **Art:** 101:4-5: on the Day of the Striking Calamity people will be like moths, dispersed, and the mountains like wool, fluffed up; then the scales are weighed, and one whose scales are heavy will be in a pleasant life. Nobody is shown: the moths are points of light, the mountains are the ones fluffed like wool, and the scales are never drawn. The look of the fluffed mountains is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A plain under a pale sky with ranks of mountains standing pale and soft, as though fluffed like wool; and around them, thousands of small lights drifting and scattering like moths in a lamplight; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 199 · An Atom's Weight · ذرة خفيفة (`atom`)
- **Ayah:** 99:7-8. **Node:** judgment.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* The Day of the Earthquake. When the earth is shaken with its final earthquake and discharges its burdens, the people depart separated, to be shown the result of their deeds. (2) *Say:* Whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it. A mustard seed's worth, a grain of dust's worth: nothing is too small to be seen. (3) **Verse 99:7-8**, placed lower.
- **Sound:** air, wind → air → air. **Art:** 99:7-8 says whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it, and whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it. The scene shows the earth opening; what each person carried is not shown, because only God knows it. The atom's weight (mithqal) is the smallest coin the Quraysh used; the coin itself is not shown.
- **Image prompt:** A bare wide plain seen from above, the earth opening into a long dark seam that widens; along it a few points of light, some warm and steady and some dim, and dust rising where it opens; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 200 · The Keepers · الرقابة (`keepers`)
- **Ayah:** 82:10-12. **Node:** judgment.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* And indeed, over you are keepers, noble and recording. They know whatever you do. (2) *Say:* Nothing of them is shown. The scene is the ground itself, and the marks scored across it, and one small light standing alone where the watching is. (3) **Verse 82:10-12**, placed lower.
- **Sound:** air, wind → air → air, wind. **Art:** 82:10-12 says that over you are keepers, noble and recording, and that they know whatever you do. The keepers are never shown: the scene is the quiet ground they watch. The look of the ground and the light is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A wide bare ground under a high sky at first light, one small pale light standing alone in the middle of it, casting a long shadow; the ground around it scored with faint parallel marks, like a page ruled for writing; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 201 · The Plea · العودة (`returnwish`)
- **Ayah:** 75:13-14. **Node:** judgment.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* And when death comes to one of them, he says: my Lord, send me back, that I might do righteousness in what I left behind. But it is only a word he is saying; and behind them is a barrier. (2) *Say:* Man will be informed that Day of what he sent ahead and kept back. Rather, man, against himself, will be a witness, even if he presents his excuses. (3) **Verse 75:13-14**, placed lower.
- **Sound:** wind, air → air → air, wind. **Art:** 23:99-100 holds the wrongdoer's own wish at the moment of death: my Lord, send me back, that I might do righteousness in what I left behind. And 75:13-14: man will be informed that Day of what he sent ahead and kept back. No one speaks on screen. The scene is the empty plain and the closed sky. The look of the sky and the light is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A bare plain at the edge of the world under an enormous still sky; one small point of light at the centre of the plain; the sky closing down slowly over it until the light is covered; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 202 · At the Collar Bones · عند الحنجرة (`soul`)
- **Ayah:** 75:26-28. **Node:** judgment.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* The Quran comes closer to a person's death than anywhere else in these chapters: when the soul has reached the collar bones, and it is said, who will cure him? (2) *Say:* The dying one is certain that it is the separation, and the leg is wound about the leg. To your Lord, that Day, is the procession. None of it is shown here; the light simply thins and goes out. (3) **Verse 75:26-28**, placed lower.
- **Sound:** wind, air → air → air, wind. **Art:** 75:26-28: when the soul has reached the collar bones, and it is said, who will cure him, and the dying one is certain that it is the separation. None of it is shown: the scene is a still plain and a light that goes out. The look of the light and the plain is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A still plain under a low sun at the end of the day, one small warm light standing alone; the light dims, thins and goes out, and the plain is left exactly as it was; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 203 · The Command Is with God · السلطان (`sovereign`)
- **Ayah:** 82:19. **Node:** judgment.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* And the Quran ends the passage like this: on that Day a soul will not possess for another soul the power to do a thing. No one can help anyone else, and no one can be persuaded to change. (2) *Say:* And the command, that Day, is entirely with God. Nothing of that is shown. The scene is the edge of the world, and the light standing at it. (3) **Verse 82:19**, placed lower.
- **Sound:** wind, air → air → air, wind. **Art:** 82:19 closes Al-Infitaar: on that Day a soul will not possess for another soul the power to do a thing, and the command, that Day, is entirely with God. God is not shown in any form; the scene is the closed horizon. The look of the horizon is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** The edge of the world: a wide empty plain running out to a hard bright line where the land meets the sky, the sky enormous and empty above it, and one small warm light at the far edge of the plain; ink-wash and parchment texture.
