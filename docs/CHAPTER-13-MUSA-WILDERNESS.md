# Chapter 13 — Musa in the Wilderness (موسى في التيه)

After the sea: manna and quails under the shading clouds, twelve springs from the rock, Musa's forty nights
on the mountain and the mountain crumbling to dust, the tablets, the golden calf and Harun's warning, Musa's
return, the yellow cow, Musa's journey with the servant of God (the boat, the boy, the wall, and the hidden
reasons), and forty years of wandering.

**Content rules kept throughout.** God is never depicted, in any form or light: on the mountain only the
mountain is seen, crumbling. Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the servant of God (al-Khidr in the
hadith) as a soft green-white light; the Samiri as a cold light. The dead man, the cow's slaughter and the
boy are told in words, never shown. The tablets carry rows of strokes, never letters. No music.

**Verses.** Every verse beat waits as `pendingVerse` (placed sky) until api.alquran.cloud can be reached;
2:57, 2:60, 7:143 and 18:82 are parts of ayat whose words are chosen by hand once fetched.

## Scenes

### 137 · Manna and Quails · طعام في الصحراء (`manna`)
- **Ayah:** 2:57. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* In the desert, God shaded them with clouds, and sent down manna and quails for them to eat. (2) *Say:* Eat of the good things We have given you, they were told. Yet many of them complained, and longed for the onions and lentils of Egypt. (3) **Verse 2:57**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** desert, voices → voices, birds → desert, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. Manna is shown as pale flakes on the ground and quails as small birds, as the Quran names them (2:57).
- **Image prompt:** A camp of goat-hair tents in a red-mountained desert under soft white clouds that shade it, pale flakes like frost settling on the sand at dawn and small birds coming down among the tents; crowds of warm lights gathering, a steady light among them; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 138 · Twelve Springs · ينابيع الصخرة (`springs`)
- **Ayah:** 2:60. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* When his people were thirsty, Musa prayed for water. Strike the rock with your staff, he was told. (2) *Say:* Twelve springs gushed out from it, one for each of the twelve tribes, and each knew its own place to drink. (3) **Verse 2:60**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** desert, voices → spring, voices → spring, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. The twelve springs and the twelve tribes are as the Quran tells (2:60, 7:160).
- **Image prompt:** A great sandstone rock in a red desert at noon, a steady white light raising a staff before it; twelve clear springs welling up in an arc around its foot, crowds of small warm lights gathering by tribe at each; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 139 · The Mountain · الجبل (`mountain`)
- **Ayah:** 7:143. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Musa went up the mountain for forty nights, to receive guidance for his people. There he asked: my Lord, show Yourself to me, that I may look at You. (2) *Say:* You will not see Me, he was told, but look at the mountain. When his Lord appeared to the mountain, it crumbled to dust, and Musa fell down unconscious. (3) *Say:* When he woke he said: glory be to You! I turn to You in repentance. Then he was given the tablets, written with guidance for his people. (4) **Verse 7:143**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind → wind, roar → air → air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. God is not depicted, in any form or light: only the mountain is seen, crumbling to dust (7:143). The tablets are drawn with rows of strokes, never letters.
- **Image prompt:** Night high in rocky mountains, a steady white light standing on a ledge before a great peak; the peak breaking apart into dust and falling stone while the light falls to its knees; then dawn, and two stone tablets standing before the light, faintly glowing; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 140 · The Golden Calf · العجل (`calf`)
- **Ayah:** 20:90. **Node:** harun.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* While Musa was on the mountain, a man called the Samiri gathered the people's gold ornaments and cast from them a calf that made a lowing sound. (2) *Say:* This is your god, and the god of Musa, he told them, and many began to worship it. (3) *Say:* Harun pleaded with them: you are only being tested by this. Your Lord is the Most Merciful, so follow me. (4) **Verse 20:90**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** crowd, fire → crowd, chant → crowd, fire → fire, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. The calf is drawn as a figure of gold, as the Quran describes (20:88); the Samiri appears only as a cold dim light.
- **Image prompt:** A desert camp at night between braziers, a calf cast of gold standing on the sand, crowds of warm lights pressing around it and a cold grey light beside it; at the edge a steady light raising his arm, pleading; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 141 · Musa's Return · رجوع موسى (`anger`)
- **Ayah:** 7:151. **Node:** harun.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Musa came back to find his people worshipping the calf. Grieved and angry, he put down the tablets and took hold of his brother. (2) *Say:* Son of my mother, said Harun, the people overpowered me and almost killed me. Do not let our enemies rejoice over me. (3) *Say:* Musa prayed: my Lord, forgive me and my brother, and take us into Your mercy. Then the calf was burned, and its ashes scattered on the wind. (4) **Verse 7:151**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, crowd → wind → fire, wind → wind, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. Nothing of the quarrel is shown beyond two lights drawn close. The calf is burned and its ashes blown away, as the Quran tells (20:97).
- **Image prompt:** The desert camp at dawn, a steady light come down from the mountain setting two stone tablets on the sand, a second steady light close before him; the golden calf burning in a fire until it is gone and its ash blows away over the sand toward the sea; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 142 · The Mountain Above Them · رفع الجبل (`liftedmount`)
- **Ayah:** 7:171. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* When Musa brought the tablets, his people hesitated to take on what was written in them. (2) *Say:* Then God raised the mountain above them, as if it were a canopy of cloud, and they were sure it would fall on them. (3) *Say:* Hold firmly to what We have given you, they were told, and remember what is in it, so that you may be mindful of God. And they made their covenant. (4) **Verse 7:171**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, crowd → wind, roar → wind → wind, air. **Art:** Musa appears only as a steady light; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. God is not depicted: only the mountain is seen, lifted like a cloud above them, as the Quran describes (7:171). That they had hesitated to accept the Torah is from the commentaries.
- **Image prompt:** A great camp of small warm lights at the foot of a rocky mountain; the whole mountain lifting silently from the earth and hanging over them like a dark cloud, its shadow across the crowd; every light bowed low; a steady light before them holding two stone tablets; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 143 · The Cow · البقرة (`cow`)
- **Ayah:** 2:69. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* A man was found dead, and no one knew who had killed him. Through Musa, God told them: slaughter a cow. (2) *Say:* Instead they kept asking: how old? what colour? what kind? until it could only be one cow: bright yellow, never yoked to plough or well. (3) *Say:* When part of it touched the dead man, God brought him to life for a moment to name his killer. So God gives life to the dead. (4) **Verse 2:69**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** herd, voices → herd, voices → voices, air → herd, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. The dead man and the slaughter of the cow are told, never shown (2:67–73).
- **Image prompt:** A camp in the desert at morning, a herd of cattle grazing among warm lights of the people; one bright yellow cow standing apart in a pool of sunlight while the others fade; a steady light among the crowd; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 144 · Qarun's Treasure · كنوز قارون (`qarun`)
- **Ayah:** 28:81. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Qarun was one of Musa's own people, given such treasures that the keys alone were a burden for a band of strong men. Do not exult, his people warned him; God does not love the boastful. (2) *Say:* I was given it because of my own knowledge, he said. When he paraded in his finery, some longed for what he had; those with knowledge said: God's reward is better. (3) *Say:* So God caused the earth to swallow him and his house, and no one could help him. (4) **Verse 28:81**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** crowd, city → crowd, voices → wind → wind, air. **Art:** Qarun and the people appear only as dim figures of light. The earth swallowing his house is shown as the house sinking into the ground; no one is shown harmed. The look of his house and treasure is imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A grand house of pale stone in a desert town, its open treasury heaped with gold glinting; a richly dressed dim light parading before crowds who gaze in envy; then the ground opening silently and the whole house sinking into the earth, dust rising, the crowd's lights shrinking back; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 145 · Where the Two Seas Meet · ملتقى البحرين (`twoseas`)
- **Ayah:** 18:65. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Musa set out with a young companion to find a servant of God at the meeting of the two seas. When they rested by a rock, their fish slipped away into the sea. (2) *Say:* That was the sign they had been seeking. Returning to the rock, they found one of God's servants, to whom He had given knowledge from Himself. (3) **Verse 18:65**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** sea, wind → sea, air → sea, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. The companion (named Yusha' in the hadith) and the servant of God (named al-Khidr in the hadith) appear only as lights; al-Khidr as a soft green-white light. The fish slipping away is shown as a small silver light.
- **Image prompt:** A rock at the meeting of two bodies of water, one blue and one grey-green, at dusk; a steady light and a smaller light of his young companion resting; a small silver light leaping from a basket into the water; then a soft green-white light waiting by the rock; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 146 · The Boat and the Wall · السفينة والجدار (`boatwall`)
- **Ayah:** 18:68. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* You will not be able to bear with me patiently, the servant of God warned. They boarded a boat, and he broke a plank in its side. Musa could not keep silent. (2) *Say:* Further on they met a boy, and the servant of God took his life. Again Musa protested; again he was reminded to be patient. (3) *Say:* In a town whose people refused them food, he rebuilt a wall that was about to fall, and asked nothing for it. (4) **Verse 18:68**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** sea, wind → sea, air → wind, work → wind, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. The servant of God appears as a soft green-white light. Of the boy, nothing is shown: his part of the story is told in words alone.
- **Image prompt:** A small wooden boat drawn up on a grey shore, a soft green-white light kneeling by its side where one plank is broken, a steady light watching in dismay; then a poor village of mud walls at dusk, a leaning wall rising straight again course by course under the soft light's hands; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 147 · The Hidden Reasons · الأسباب الخفية (`reasons`)

(2) *Say:* The boy would have brought grief and disbelief upon his believing parents; God would give them a better child, purer and more loving. (3) *Say:* And under the wall lay a treasure belonging to two orphans, whose father had been a righteous man; it would wait for them until they came of age. (4) **Verse 18:82**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** sea, wind → air → wind, air → air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. The servant of God appears as a soft green-white light. The king's ships, the parents and the orphans' treasure are shown only as he explains them.
- **Image prompt:** Three quiet scenes: a grey sea with a line of a king's dark ships taking every sound boat, while one damaged boat is left on the shore; a mother and father of light with a new small light between them; a straightened village wall with a faint gold glow of buried treasure beneath it; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 148 · Forty Years in the Wilderness · التيه (`forty`)
- **Ayah:** 5:26. **Node:** musa.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Musa brought them to the edge of the holy land, but they were afraid of the people there and refused to enter: go, you and your Lord, and fight. We will sit here. (2) *Say:* So for forty years they wandered in the desert, until a new generation grew up. (3) **Verse 5:26**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, voices → wind, desert → wind, air. **Art:** Musa and Harun appear only as steady lights; the Children of Israel as small warm lights. The look of the wilderness is imagined. Of Musa's and Harun's deaths nothing is shown; the chapter closes on the wandering.
- **Image prompt:** The edge of a green land seen across a desert from a ridge, a great camp of warm lights halted before it; then the desert under a sky racing through day and night again and again as the camp moves in slow circles across the sand; two steady lights at its head; ink-wash and parchment texture.
