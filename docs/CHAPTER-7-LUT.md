# Chapter 7 — Prophet Lut (لوط)

The chapter after Ibrahim's: the angels who were his guests go on to the cities of the plain, where his
nephew Lut had warned his people for years. The cities' wrongdoing, their threat to drive Lut out, the
angels' arrival, the crowd at Lut's door, the family's flight by night, the morning the cities were
overturned, and the salt sea left as a sign. Scenes are in `src/story/chapters/c7/`; image prompts follow
the shared Troy + Three Kingdoms style.

**Content rules kept throughout.** God is never depicted. Lut appears only as a steady light, far away;
the angels only as tall figures of radiance, never as men; his family and the people as dim or small
lights; no faces. The people's deeds are named in plain, modest words and never shown; their scorn is
drawn as jagged strokes. Nothing violent is shown: when the punishment comes, the cities' lights go out
and the land turns over. No music.

**Engine used here.** Cards turned over by their `move` (`rot`), drawn on both faces; crowds whose lights
go out (`out`); dust laid over the scene (`blend: "normal"`) for smoke and falling stones; a flash and
the stage's shake for the blast.

## Scenes

### 79 · The Cities of the Plain · مدن السهل (`plain`)
- **Ayah:** 29:28. **Engine:** a walled city on a plain by a dark sea; a lamplit square with a milling crowd and strokes of mockery.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Lut, Ibrahim's nephew, had been sent as a messenger to the cities of the plain beside the sea. (2) *Say:* Their people robbed travellers on the roads and did shameful things openly, in their gatherings. (3) *Say:* Lut called them to fear God and to leave what they did. (4) **Verse 29:28**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, water → crowd, jeer → crowd, voice → crowd, air. **Art:** The cities are imagined beside the Dead Sea, where tradition places them; that Lut was Ibrahim's nephew is said in the commentaries. The people appear as dim lights and their mockery as jagged strokes; their deeds are named, never shown. Lut appears only as a steady light, far away.
- **Image prompt:** Two walled cities of flat-roofed mud brick on a green plain beside a still, dark inland sea at dusk, pink cliffs across the water, lamps coming alight; then a lamplit square at night crowded with dim amber lights, jagged dark-red strokes of mockery swirling over them, and one steady white light standing among them with an arm raised; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 80 · Drive Them Out · التهديد بالطرد (`expel`)
- **Ayah:** 27:56. **Engine:** a lamplit doorway at night and a city gate by day (two sets).
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Year after year Lut warned them; only his own household believed, and not all of them. (2) *Say:* They mocked him: bring God's punishment down on us, if you are telling the truth. (3) **Verse 27:56**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** air, crickets → crowd, jeer → jeer, crowd. **Art:** Lut appears only as a steady light and his household as small lights within; the people as dim lights, their mockery as jagged strokes. The house and the gate are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A narrow street of dark mud-brick walls at night, one small doorway glowing with lamplight and a few small lights inside, a steady white light standing before it; then a city gate of brick with squat towers in hard daylight, a crowd of dim amber lights before it and jagged red strokes of mockery in the air; then night again, the crowd pressing at the glowing doorway; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 81 · The Visitors · الزائرون (`visitors`)
- **Ayah:** 11:77. **Engine:** radiances walking a road into the distance and through a gateway.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Then the angels who had been Ibrahim's guests came on to Lut's city, in the form of young men. (2) *Say:* Lut went out to them, troubled for their sake, for he knew his people. (3) **Verse 11:77**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, air → air, city → air, city. **Art:** The angels appear only as tall figures of radiance, never as men; that they came in the form of young men is said in the commentaries. Lut appears only as a steady light, far away.
- **Image prompt:** Late afternoon on a road through green fields toward the brick gate of a walled city by a dark inland sea; three tall figures of pure white radiance walking up the road toward the gate; a steady white light coming out through the gate to meet them; long gold light, pink cliffs beyond the water; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 82 · At the Door · عند الباب (`door`)
- **Ayah:** 54:37. **Engine:** a crowd streaming down a receding street; the crowd faltering and scattering as its lights blur.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* When the people heard of his guests, they came rushing to his house. (2) *Say:* Lut stood at his door and pleaded with them: fear God, and do not disgrace me before my guests. (3) *Say:* Then the guests told Lut that they were messengers from his Lord, and that the crowd would never reach him; and the crowd's eyes were struck blind. (4) **Verse 54:37**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** crowd, jeer → crowd, jeer → air → air, wind. **Art:** The angels appear only as radiance and Lut as a steady light; the crowd as dim lights and its demands as jagged strokes. The blinding is shown only as their lights faltering and scattering. The street and the house are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** A narrow street of dark mud-brick houses at night, a stream of restless dim amber lights pouring down it toward one lamplit doorway at the end, jagged red strokes of anger in the air; then the doorway close: a steady white light standing in it with an arm raised, the crowd pressing; then two tall figures of pure white radiance appearing behind him and a wave of light washing over the street, the crowd's lights faltering and scattering; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 83 · By Night · الخروج ليلاً (`night`)
- **Ayah:** 15:65. **Engine:** a file of lights passing out through a gate; one light turning back.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Leave in the last part of the night with your family, the angels told him, and let none of you look back. (2) *Say:* But his wife stayed behind; her heart was with her people. (3) **Verse 15:65**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** air, crickets → wind, crickets → wind. **Art:** Lut appears only as a steady light, his family as small lights and the angels as radiance. That his wife remained behind is told in the Quran; it is shown only as her light turning back. The city and the road are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** Deep night inside a walled city, a small file of lights led by two tall figures of radiance passing out through the dark gateway under the stars; then from a hill road above the dark city by its black sea, the little line of lights climbing away while one dim amber light has stopped and turned back down toward the city; indigo, silver, faint amber; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 84 · The Morning · ذلك الصباح (`morning`)
- **Ayah:** 11:82. **Engine:** cities lifting and turning over (a card turned by its move) as their lights go out; dust; stones of clay.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* At sunrise, the command came. (2) *Say:* A mighty blast seized them, and their cities were turned upside down. (3) *Say:* And stones of baked clay rained down upon them, layer on layer. (4) **Verse 11:82**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, water → storm → storm, wind → wind. **Art:** No one is shown suffering: the cities' lights go out and the land is turned over, as the Quran describes; the stones are streaks of clay red. The cities by the Dead Sea are imagined.
- **Image prompt:** Dawn over two walled cities of mud brick on a plain beside a still inland sea, their lamps still burning; then a blinding flash, the ground heaving, the cities lifting and turning over into the earth as their lights go out, dust rolling up; then streaks of clay-red stones raining down through the smoke; finally a dark, empty plain under a bruised sky; ink-wash and parchment texture.

### 85 · A Clear Sign · البحر الميت (`sign`)
- **Ayah:** 29:35. **Engine:** a salt sea with white crusts and pillars; travellers on the shore road.
- **Beats.** (1) *Say:* Where the cities stood, tradition says, lies a sea so heavy with salt that nothing lives in it, at the lowest place on the face of the earth. (2) *Say:* The road beside it is still travelled. (3) *Say:* Lut and those who believed with him were saved. (4) **Verse 29:35**, placed sky.
- **Sound:** wind, water → wind, desert → wind, air → wind. **Art:** That the cities lay where the Dead Sea is now is the traditional identification; the salt sea shown is the present-day one. Lut appears only as a steady light, far away, his family as small lights; the travellers as lights.
- **Image prompt:** A still, heavy inland sea of milky turquoise beneath pink and ochre cliffs, white salt crusted along its shore in cracked plates and rising in pale pillars, heat haze; at dusk a few small lights of travellers passing along the shore road; then on a far hilltop at sunset a steady white light and two small amber lights looking out over the land; ink-wash and parchment texture.

## What the engine shows and what it approximates

- The overturning is a painted city lifted and turned over on its card, then sunk below the plain; the
  stones are streaks of clay red in the air, not modelled stones.
- The crowd's blinding is shown only as its lights blurring, dimming and scattering.
